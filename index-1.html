Let's go back to where it started.

It began at Tea Post. You were shy for a straight half hour, and I loved every quiet minute of it. A sandwich, ice tea, hot tea and khakhra with dip did the talking until you found your words.

You came with an orchid stem and a handmade crochet sunflower. I came with two real ones. Turns out we both had sunflowers on our minds.

Then Law Garden. More than 10,000 steps, and we talked about everything. Somewhere in there, the shyness left and the stories stayed.

Ice cream, because some evenings deserve a sweet ending.

You dropped me to the metro station. I rode home with an orchid in my hand and a smile I couldn't fix.

The real sunflowers have wilted by now. The crochet one hasn't. Neither have we.

A love letter.

Mahi,

Dhup, chaanv, sardi, baarish — sab mausam dekhe tere saath, lekin best wale din hamesha wahi hote hai jab tujhe milti hu.

Wo chote bachhe ko promise karo na ki mai tumhe chocolate dilaungi, aur wo kitna khush ho jaata hai. Tere se ek call ke baad mai uss chote bachhe se lakh guna zyada khush ho jaati hu. Har cheez achieveable lagti hai, pata hai, tere aane ke baad. Kabhi kabar sochti hu ki mount everest chadne ka bhi ho na, to bhi ho jayega.

Likhne ke liye to 100 panne bhi kam padd jaye tere baare mai, lekin sabme ghum fir ke mai yehi bol dungi ki bass tera hona acha lagta hai.

Thank you for always being there — pyaar karne ke liye, cheezein sikhane, samjhane ke liye, mera personal singer banne ke liye, mere saath bakchodiya karne ke liye. Tujhse baat na ho na to din adhura lagta hai, kuch karne ka mann nahi karta.

Mujhe pata hai I am not the easiest girlfriend to be with. I am sorry for that, but thank you kabhi aisa feel na karane ke liye ki I am hard to be with.

Baaki to yaar tujhe pata hi hai, mai teri hi hu.

— Laksha
